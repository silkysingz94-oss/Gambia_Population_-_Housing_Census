Methodology
1973–2003: historical population and density use LGA totals and the embedded historical LGA area values. EA boundaries are used only to display the LGA-level results geographically.

2013 EA population: sum_POP.
2024 EA population: enum_pop.
EA density: EA population divided by Area_sqkm.

Households: sum_HHS (2013) and enum_hhs (2024).
Compounds: Point_Count (2013) and comp_map (2024).

GeoJSON coordinates are converted from EPSG:3857 to EPSG:4326 for Leaflet display.
