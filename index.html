Methodology and Data Notes

Historical population

1973, 1983, 1993, 2003 and 2013 figures are taken from the supplied historical table and are not recalculated from the GeoJSON.

2024 census layer

The GitHub GeoJSON provides the spatial data and available 2024 attributes. Feature values are read using recognised population and LGA property names.

Annual population growth

Calculated as the compound annual growth rate between census observations when both population values are available.

Population density

Population divided by the corresponding area in square kilometres. Confirm area units and projection before official reporting.

Missing information

Missing census values are not interpolated or invented. The dashboard distinguishes unavailable figures from zero population.
