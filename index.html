Methodology and Data Notes

Historical population

1973, 1983, 1993, 2003 and 2013 figures are taken from the supplied historical table and are not recalculated from the GeoJSON.

2024 census layer

The GitHub GeoJSON provides the spatial data and available 2024 attributes. Feature values are read using recognised population and LGA property names.

2024 LGA area calculation

Area is calculated by summing the areas of EA polygons assigned to each LGA. Turf.js geometry area is used where a suitable area attribute is unavailable. This assumes the polygons do not overlap or duplicate one another.

Annual population growth

Calculated as the compound annual growth rate between 2013 and 2024 when both population values are available.

Population density

Population divided by the corresponding area in square kilometres. Density is calculated separately for each LGA.

Missing information

Missing census values are not interpolated or invented. The dashboard distinguishes unavailable figures from zero population.
