Methodology and notes
2024 EA analysis: Population is aggregated from enum_pop, households from enum_hhs and compounds from comp_map. Population density is calculated as aggregated enum_pop divided by the sum of Area_sqkm for the selected EA features. All eight LGAs are included in the aggregation where records exist. EA boundaries are displayed from the uploaded GeoJSON.
